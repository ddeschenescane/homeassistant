Residential monitoring and notification service

About the Service

43 Sherwood Monitoring provides automated notifications to authorized family members regarding monitoring, security, safety, and other configured events occurring at a designated residential property.

Notifications are informational and are generated by configured monitoring events. The service is intended for a small group of authorized recipients who have voluntarily opted in to receive notifications.

SMS Notifications

Authorized recipients may choose to receive SMS notifications when monitoring events require attention. Message frequency varies depending on monitoring activity.

Message and data rates may apply. Recipients may stop receiving SMS notifications at any time by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP.
