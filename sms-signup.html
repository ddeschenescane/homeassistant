Enroll for SMS Notifications

Authorized family members may voluntarily enroll to receive SMS notifications from 43 Sherwood Monitoring regarding monitoring, security, safety, and other configured events occurring at a designated residential property.

Messages are informational and are triggered by monitoring events. Message frequency varies based on monitoring activity.

Message and data rates may apply. You may stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance.

Open SMS Enrollment Form

About SMS Consent

SMS enrollment requires you to provide your mobile phone number and separately provide affirmative consent to receive monitoring notifications.

SMS consent is voluntary and is separate from acceptance of the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy. Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent information are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

By enrolling, you confirm that you control the mobile phone number provided and are requesting these monitoring notifications for yourself.
