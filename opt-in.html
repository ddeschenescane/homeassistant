Call to Action / Opt-In Process

Recipients provide their mobile phone number directly to the account owner for the purpose of receiving personal text messages from the account owner.

There is no public advertising campaign, web form, or lead-generation process associated with this messaging service. Recipients are personally known to the account owner and voluntarily provide their phone number and agree to receive text messages.

The account owner does not send messages to individuals who have not provided their phone number and agreed to receive communications.

Recipients may stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP. Recipients may also contact the account owner directly to request that their number be removed from the messaging list.
