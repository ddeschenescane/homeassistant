Privacy Policy Last updated: September 8, 2026 This service is used for personal, non-commercial text messaging between the account owner and a small group of family members. Information We Collect The service may collect and store mobile phone numbers and message-related information necessary to send and receive text messages. How We Use Information Mobile phone numbers are used solely to communicate with family members by text message. We do not use phone numbers for advertising or marketing purposes. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Text Messaging By providing a phone number and agreeing to receive text messages, a recipient consents to receive personal communications from the account owner. Message frequency varies depending on family communication needs. Message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's mobile carrier and plan. Recipients may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. After receiving an opt-out request, no further messages will be sent unless the recipient subsequently provides consent to receive them again. For assistance, recipients may reply HELP or contact the account owner directly. Data Retention Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the messaging service and maintain appropriate records. Third-Party Services The service may use third-party telecommunications providers to transmit text messages. Those providers process information as necessary to deliver the messages.
