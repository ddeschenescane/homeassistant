Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, protected, and retained in connection with the SMS monitoring notification service operated by David Deschenes ("Service Operator").

Information We Collect

The service may collect and process information necessary to provide and maintain SMS monitoring notifications, including:

  • Mobile telephone numbers
  • SMS message content
  • Message delivery and status information
  • Information associated with monitoring and notification events
  • SMS opt-in, consent, and opt-out information
  • Information provided when recipients request assistance or support

We collect only information reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and support the SMS monitoring notification service.

How We Use Information

Mobile telephone numbers and related information are used to deliver SMS monitoring notifications that recipients have requested or authorized.

Information may be used to:

  • Send requested SMS monitoring notifications
  • Process and track message delivery and status
  • Respond to HELP or support requests
  • Process STOP and other opt-out requests
  • Maintain records necessary to operate and troubleshoot the service
  • Maintain records of SMS consent and opt-in
  • Maintain records of opt-out requests
  • Protect the security and reliability of the service
  • Comply with applicable legal and telecommunications requirements

We do not use mobile telephone numbers or SMS consent information for unrelated advertising, promotional, or marketing purposes.

SMS Consent

Recipients provide their mobile telephone number and explicitly consent before being enrolled to receive SMS monitoring notifications.

SMS consent is voluntary and is specific to the monitoring notification service described in this Privacy Policy.

Mobile telephone numbers, SMS opt-in information, and messaging consent will not be sold, rented, transferred, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Message frequency varies depending on the monitoring events and notifications configured for the service.

Message and data rates may apply.

Service Providers

The service uses third-party telecommunications, messaging, cloud, and technology providers to operate and deliver SMS notifications. These providers may process mobile telephone numbers, message content, delivery information, and other information necessary to provide their services.

Such providers process information on behalf of the service or as necessary to provide the telecommunications and technology services used by the monitoring system. They are not authorized by the Service Operator to use mobile telephone numbers or SMS opt-in information for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information.

We do not sell, rent, or provide mobile telephone numbers to third parties or affiliates for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

We do not sell, rent, or share SMS opt-in information or messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, property, or operation of the service.

Opt-Out and SMS Preferences

Recipients may stop receiving SMS monitoring notifications at any time by replying STOP to an SMS message.

After receiving an opt-out request, the recipient's mobile number will be removed from the active SMS notification list. Information relating to the opt-in or opt-out may be retained when reasonably necessary for legal, compliance, security, or recordkeeping purposes.

Recipients may resume receiving SMS monitoring notifications by providing new consent through the service.

Help

Recipients may reply HELP to an SMS message for assistance.

Recipients may also contact the Service Operator using the contact information provided below.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, provide requested notifications, maintain delivery and compliance records, resolve disputes, maintain security, and satisfy legal or regulatory requirements.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or securely disposed of.

Data Security

Reasonable administrative, physical, and technical measures are used to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Accordingly, the Service Operator cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted to or stored by the service.

Children's Privacy

This service is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
