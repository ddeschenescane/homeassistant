Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This service is used for personal, non-commercial text messaging between the account owner and a small group of family members.

Information We Collect

The service may collect and store mobile phone numbers and message-related information necessary to send and receive text messages.

How We Use Information

Mobile phone numbers are used solely to communicate with family members by text message.

We do not use phone numbers for advertising or marketing purposes. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Text Messaging

By voluntarily providing a mobile phone number and agreeing to receive text messages, a recipient consents to receive personal communications from the account owner.

Messages may include family updates, reminders, scheduling information, event coordination, and other personal communications.

Message frequency varies depending on family communication needs. Message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's mobile carrier and plan.

Recipients may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. After receiving an opt-out request, no further messages will be sent unless the recipient subsequently provides consent to receive them again.

For assistance, recipients may reply HELP or contact the account owner directly.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the messaging service and maintain appropriate records.

Third-Party Services

This service uses third-party telecommunications providers to transmit text messages. These providers may process phone numbers, message content, and related information as necessary to deliver messages.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or text-message communications may be directed to the account owner.
