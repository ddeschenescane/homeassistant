Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and protected in connection with the SMS notification service operated by the account owner.

Information We Collect

The service may collect and process information necessary to provide SMS notifications, including:

  • Mobile telephone numbers
  • SMS message content
  • Message delivery and status information
  • Information associated with notification events
  • SMS opt-in and consent information

We collect only information necessary to operate and maintain the messaging service.

How We Use Information

Mobile telephone numbers and related information are used to deliver SMS notifications that recipients have requested or authorized.

Information may be used to:

  • Send requested SMS notifications
  • Process and track message delivery
  • Respond to HELP or support requests
  • Process STOP and other opt-out requests
  • Maintain records necessary to operate and troubleshoot the service
  • Maintain records of SMS consent and opt-in
  • Comply with applicable legal and telecommunications requirements

We do not use mobile telephone numbers or SMS consent information for unrelated advertising or marketing purposes.

SMS Consent

Recipients provide their mobile telephone number and consent before being enrolled to receive SMS notifications.

SMS consent is voluntary and is specific to the notification service described in this policy.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not sold, rented, transferred, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

Message frequency varies depending on the notifications configured for the service.

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out

Recipients may stop receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to a message.

After receiving an opt-out request, the recipient's number will be removed from the active SMS notification list, except where information must be retained for legal, compliance, or recordkeeping purposes.

Recipients may resume receiving messages by providing new consent through the service.

Help

Recipients may reply HELP to an SMS message for assistance.

Recipients may also contact the service operator using the contact information provided below.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or provide mobile telephone numbers to third parties or affiliates for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

We do not sell, rent, or share SMS opt-in information or messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

The service uses telecommunications and technology providers to deliver SMS messages. These providers may process telephone numbers, message content, delivery information, and other information necessary to provide their services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or operation of the service.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, provide requested notifications, maintain delivery and compliance records, resolve disputes, and satisfy legal or regulatory requirements.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or securely disposed of.

Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

This service is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
